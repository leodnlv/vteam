A new 5 Stats tab in the TUI. Each screen below is rendered at 104 columns using the colors from internal/palette. t switches between the last 24h and the last 7 days (you can also click the buttons). The numbers are mock data shaped like the real vteam/.runs, logs, and aven tasks.

Option B rebuilt from fields vteam already records. Triage time, todo time, and merge time aren't stored, so the funnel becomes "where are this window's findings now" (aven created_at + current status + merged=true). Lead time only shows spans with a timestamp at both ends.
